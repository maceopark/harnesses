# Wave 2 — Assurance Warrants

- Current `warrant` and `counterevidence` are opaque prose; readiness counts eligible groups without machine-checking support applicability or open defeaters.
- Conditional proposal: strict per-ledger AssuranceFragment linking claim digest, evidence, context, assumptions, warrant, targeted defeaters, revision, and change triggers.
- Readiness requires eligible evidence plus an undefeated applicable support relation; unresolved defeaters propagate unsupported.
- Semantic warrant adequacy, exhaustive defeater coverage, and acceptable residual risk remain human boundaries.
