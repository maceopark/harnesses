# Wave 2 — External Verifier Routing

- Intrinsic critique proposes and counter-searches only.
- Fallible tools/learned verifiers may challenge/localize but cannot independently settle high-impact facts.
- Only a current, sound, scoped checker may authorize its exact encoded predicate; it does not establish spec adequacy/open-world completeness.
- Fail closed or abstain when checker scope/input/version/freshness is unknown; retries are bounded.
- EXPAND: verifier-class fields, gate mutation, historical verifier benchmark, cross-vendor correlation measurement.
