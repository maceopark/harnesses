# Wave 1 — Requirements and Human Factors

Worker: `/root/requirements_human` · Observed: 2026-07-10

## Digest

- Interviews produce bounded qualitative evidence, not completeness or population validity.
- NASA validation combines technical correctness, traceability, assumptions, stakeholder coverage, feasibility, verifiability, and nonredundancy.
- Assurance cases need claim, warrant/strategy, evidence, context, assumptions, and defeaters; notation does not establish truth.
- Reviewer independence is informational/incentive/expertise independence, not count.
- Falsification should discriminate live alternatives; ritual negative questioning is not justified.
