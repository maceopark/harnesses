# Wave 3 — Crypto Transfer Audit

- Exact locally: encoded witness predicate satisfaction, deterministic gate conformance, audit record existence, and conceptual separation of support/refutation/adequacy.
- Bounded only: freeze-before-challenge, independent discriminating probes, repetition, verifier/prover work asymmetry.
- Reject guarantee transfer: cryptographic completeness/soundness, PoK/extraction, ZK, Fiat-Shamir, rewinding, numeric amplification.
- Formation-vs-test must be explicit because requirements may be co-created, not pre-existing hidden witnesses.
- Safe final language: evidence-backed acceptance under recorded evidence/scope/assumptions/environment/time; never global truth/completeness/independence/extractability.
