# Clarify Requirements

Restate the outcome and inspect authorized context for answers. A decision is material if alternatives change observable behavior, scope, safety, compatibility, cost, reversibility, or acceptance.

Track material decisions as confirmed, inferred, or open. Ask the highest-leverage open decision first and ask one bounded question per turn. Explain why it matters and offer concrete options when useful. Never silently default an open material decision. Skip low-impact preferences.

Stop when all material decisions are confirmed or explicitly approved assumptions and acceptance is testable. Return scope, decisions, behavior, constraints, acceptance checks, open non-blockers, and whether implementation is authorized. Reflect it back for approval.
